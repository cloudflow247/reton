The problem

Sending money is easy.
Taking it back is not.

  • One wrong digit and the money is gone.
  • Scams move faster than bank disputes.
  • Most wallets optimise for “send and forget.”
  • People don’t need another clone — they need a second chance.

Why this is real

When money leaves…
getting it back is a war.

In 2024, about ₦95 million was pulled from a company’s account without permission. Banks rushed “block and recall.” Freezes often last only 72 hours. A court later ordered the money returned — and still, reversing it became a legal fight.

How it works today

Money flies out → hope a freeze works → race the clock → maybe a court → maybe nothing comes back.

What a baby can understand

If you give away a toy and it leaves the room, chasing it is hard. Reton keeps a string on the toy until you say “okay.”

Reported widely in Nigerian media (e.g. GWG, May 2025) — the pain is public. The product gap is still open.

Reton’s answer

Don’t wait for a chase.
Build the undo first.

  • Callback Protection — hold the payment before it becomes final.
  • Wrong-transfer recovery — freeze eligible funds and open a case when something goes wrong.
  • Fraud signals — watch risky moves while money is still in motion.
  • A clear timeline — so everyone can see what happened, step by step.

Courts and bank recalls still matter. Reton adds something they can’t: protection inside the payment itself.

The insight

Trust isn’t a badge on the login screen. It’s what happens after the money moves.

Reton is built for the moment something goes wrong — not just the moment you hit send.
